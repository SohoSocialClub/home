PRIVACY POLICY

PURPOSE OF PROCESSING

Personal data provided through the registration form will be processed for the following purposes:

  • Managing your membership application
  • Maintaining an up-to-date record of members
  • Communications related to club activities
  • Compliance with legal obligations on data protection

LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

Processing of your data is carried out under the following legal bases:

  • Explicit consent of the data subject, provided by marking the corresponding checkbox in the registration form
  • Compliance with legal obligations arising from association and data protection regulations

DATA COLLECTED

The data requested in the registration form is:

  • Full name
  • DNI/NIE/Passport
  • Date of birth
  • Contact phone number
  • Email address
  • Full postal address
  • Membership start date

All fields marked as mandatory (*) are required to process your membership application.

RETENTION PERIOD

Personal data will be kept for the time necessary to manage your membership status and for as long as there is a legal obligation to keep them. Once authorization is revoked or the relationship with the club ends, data will be deleted within a maximum of 30 days, unless a legal requirement mandates longer retention.

DATA RECIPIENTS

Your data will not be disclosed to third parties, except where required by law. Form hosting and management is carried out through Formspree (Formspree IO, Inc.), which acts as a data processor under the GDPR. You can consult Formspree's privacy policy at: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy

DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

At any time you may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing, and data portability, by sending an email to privacidad@malagasocialclub.es, attaching a copy of your ID.

  • Access: right to obtain confirmation as to whether your data is being processed
  • Rectification: right to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: right to obtain the deletion of your data
  • Objection: right to object to processing
  • Restriction: right to limit the use of your data
  • Portability: right to receive your data in a structured format

COMPLAINT TO THE SUPERVISORY AUTHORITY

If you consider that the processing of your data does not respect your privacy or violates the regulations, you have the right to file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD): www.aepd.es

SECURITY MEASURES

The club applies appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure the security and integrity of your personal data, protecting it against unauthorized or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, or damage.

UPDATES
