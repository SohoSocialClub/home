BYLAWS

TITLE I — NAME AND PURPOSES

Article 1. The association is named Málaga Social Club, constituted as a private non-profit entity under Organic Law 1/2002 of March 22, regulated under Association Law.

Article 2. The club's purposes are the promotion of responsible, safe, and respectful cannabis use, information and prevention, and the individual and collective well-being of its members. No sale or distribution of narcotics takes place under any circumstances.

TITLE II — MEMBERS

Article 3. Persons over 21 years of age who share the values of the club and meet the conditions established in these bylaws may become members.

Article 4. Membership is personal and non-transferable. Each member has one vote in collective decision-making.

Article 5. Members' personal data shall be treated confidentially, solely for internal administrative purposes, in accordance with the LOPDGDD and GDPR.

TITLE III — BODIES

Article 6. The club's bodies are: the General Assembly (supreme body) and the Board of Directors. The Assembly meets at least once a year in ordinary session.

Article 7. The Board of Directors is composed of a minimum of three members elected by voting in the Assembly, with a term of two years.

TITLE IV — CONSUMPTION RULES

Article 8. Cannabis consumption within the club's facilities is exclusive to members, within the framework of activities authorized by current legislation.

Article 9. Redistribution to third parties of any cannabis product acquired at the club is strictly prohibited. Consumption is exclusively personal.

Article 10. Access by persons under 21 is not permitted, nor is the consumption of other illicit substances within the club's facilities.

TITLE V — ECONOMIC REGIME

Article 11. The club is financed through periodic membership fees and voluntary contributions. The amount of the fees is approved by the General Assembly.

Article 12. The dissolution of the club requires the favorable vote of two thirds of the General Assembly, appointing liquidators who will make effective the remaining assets after meeting obligations.

TITLE VI — DISCIPLINARY REGIME

Article 13. Minor infractions shall be sanctioned with a warning; serious ones, with suspension of between one and twelve months; very serious ones, with expulsion. All of this prior to a hearing procedure for the person concerned.

Article 14. Very serious infractions include: redistribution to third parties, access by minors, and any conduct that compromises the security of the club.

TITLE VII — FINAL PROVISIONS

Article 15. These bylaws may be amended by an Extraordinary General Assembly, convened for this purpose at least 15 days in advance.

Article 16. Whatever is not provided for in these bylaws shall be governed by Organic Law 1/2002 of March 22, on Associations, and other concordant regulations.
